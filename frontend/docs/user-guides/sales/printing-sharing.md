# 🖨️ Printing & Sharing Invoices

> Learn how to print, download, and share your invoices.

---

## Print Options

After creating an invoice, you have several options:

| Option | Best For |
|--------|----------|
| **Print** | Physical copy |
| **Download PDF** | Digital records |
| **WhatsApp** | Send to customer instantly |
| **Email** | Formal communication |

---

## Printing an Invoice

### Print Immediately After Saving
1. Save the invoice
2. Success popup appears
3. Click **Print** button
4. Select printer and print

### Print from Invoice History
1. Go to **Sales → Invoice History**
2. Find the invoice
3. Click the **🖨️ Print** icon
4. Or open invoice and click Print

---

## Print Sizes

We support multiple print formats:

| Size | Use Case |
|------|----------|
| **Thermal 2"** | Small receipt printers |
| **Thermal 3"** | Standard POS printers |
| **A5** | Half page invoices |
| **A4** | Full page invoices |

### Change Print Size
1. Go to **Settings → Print Templates**
2. Select your preferred size
3. This becomes the default

---

## Download as PDF

For digital records:

1. Open the invoice
2. Click **Download** or **PDF** icon
3. PDF is saved to your device
4. Can be shared via any method

---

## Share via WhatsApp

Send invoice directly to customer's WhatsApp:

1. Open the invoice
2. Click **WhatsApp** icon
3. Enter customer's phone number (or use saved number)
4. Message opens with PDF attachment
5. Send

> 💡 **Tip**: Customer's phone is auto-filled if saved in their profile.

---

## Send via Email

For formal communication:

1. Open the invoice
2. Click **Email** icon
3. Enter or confirm email address
4. Optional: Add a message
5. Click Send

---

## Print Queue

Print multiple invoices at once:

1. Go to **Sales → Invoice History**
2. Select multiple invoices (checkboxes)
3. Click **Print Selected**
4. All invoices print one after another

---

## Customizing Print Layout

Change what appears on your invoices:

1. Go to **Settings → Print Templates**
2. Choose template to edit
3. Options include:
   - Show/hide company logo
   - Show/hide bank details
   - Terms & conditions text
   - Footer message
   - Signature space

4. Save template

---

## Common Questions

### Why is my print layout different?
Check **Settings → Print Templates** to ensure correct template is selected.

### Can I add my logo?
Yes! Go to **Settings → Company Profile** and upload your logo.

### Print quality is poor?
- Check printer quality settings
- Use correct paper size
- Try downloading PDF and printing from a PDF reader

### How do I print without GST columns?
For non-GST or B2C bills, change to appropriate template in Print Settings.

---

## Best Practices

✅ **Test print first** - Before customer transactions  
✅ **Keep digital copies** - Download PDFs for backup  
✅ **Use WhatsApp** - Faster and saves paper  
✅ **Check template** - Ensure right size is selected
