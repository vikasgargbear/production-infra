# 💰 Managing Outstanding Payments

> Track customer dues and collect pending payments effectively.

---

## What is Outstanding?

**Outstanding** refers to money customers owe you for credit sales. This guide helps you:

- View who owes you money
- See how long payments are overdue
- Collect payments efficiently
- Send payment reminders

---

## Viewing Outstanding Summary

1. Go to **Sales → Outstanding** or **Dashboard → Pending Payments**
2. You'll see a summary:
   - **Total Outstanding**: All money owed to you
   - **Overdue Amount**: Past due date
   - **Current Amount**: Not yet due

---

## Understanding the Outstanding List

For each customer, you'll see:

| Column | What it Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Customer Name** | Customer/business name |
| **Phone** | Contact number |
| **Total Outstanding** | Total amount owed |
| **Overdue** | Amount past due date |
| **Pending Invoices** | Number of unpaid invoices |
| **Oldest Invoice** | Days since oldest unpaid |

> 💡 **Tip**: Red amounts indicate overdue payments.

---

## Viewing Customer Details

Click on any customer to see:

### Invoice Breakdown
- List of all unpaid invoices
- Invoice date and due date
- Original amount and balance
- Days overdue

### Payment History
- Previous payments received
- When and how they paid

---

## Collecting Payments

### From Outstanding Screen

1. Click on customer name
2. Click **Receive Payment**
3. Enter payment amount
4. Select payment method (Cash/Card/UPI/etc.)
5. Click **Save Payment**

### Partial Payments
- Enter any amount up to total outstanding
- Payment is allocated to oldest invoices first
- Balance remains outstanding

### Full Payment
- Enter the full outstanding amount
- All invoices are marked as paid

---

## Aging Analysis

The aging report shows how old your receivables are:

| Aging Bucket | Description |
|--------------|-------------|
| **Current** | Not yet due |
| **1-30 Days** | 1 to 30 days overdue |
| **31-60 Days** | 31 to 60 days overdue |
| **61-90 Days** | 61 to 90 days overdue |
| **Over 90 Days** | More than 90 days overdue |

> 💡 **Tip**: Focus on collecting 60+ days overdue amounts first.

## Sending Payment Reminders

### Via WhatsApp
1. Click on customer
2. Click **Send Reminder** or WhatsApp icon
3. A pre-formatted message with dues opens
4. Send to customer

### Via SMS/Email
- Configure in Settings → Notifications
- Send individual or bulk reminders

## Filtering Outstanding

Use filters to focus on specific cases:

| Filter | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **All** | All outstanding |
| **Overdue** | Only past due date |
| **High Value** | Above ₹10,000 (adjustable) |
| **Oldest First** | Sorted by oldest invoice |

## Setting Credit Limits

Control how much credit to extend:

1. Go to **Customers → Edit Customer**
2. Set **Credit Limit** (e.g., ₹50,000)
3. Set **Credit Days** (e.g., 30 days)
4. Save

When limit is reached:
- Warning appears during new sale
- Optional: Block new credit sales

---

## Exporting Outstanding Data

For records or follow-up:

1. Go to **Sales → Outstanding**
2. Click **Export**
3. Choose format (Excel, PDF)
4. Data downloads

You can:
- Share with team for collections
- Print for manual tracking
- Import to other systems

## Common Questions

### How do I write off bad debt?
Go to **Finance → Journal Entry** and create a write-off entry. The outstanding will be reduced.

### Can I adjust an outstanding amount?
Yes, create an adjustment through **Finance → Adjustments** with proper documentation.

### Why is my outstanding different from reports?
Check date ranges - Outstanding shows current dues, reports may show historical data.

### How do advance payments work?
Advance payments create a negative outstanding (credit balance). They are applied to future invoices.

## Best Practices

✅ **Review weekly** - Check aging report regularly  
✅ **Follow up promptly** - Remind at 7, 15, 30 days  
✅ **Document promises** - Note when customer promises to pay  
✅ **Set credit limits** - Prevent excessive credit  
✅ **Offer incentives** - Early payment discounts
