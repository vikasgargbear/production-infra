# 💸 Applying Discounts

> Learn all the ways to give discounts to your customers.

---

## Types of Discounts

| Type | Applied To | When to Use |
|------|-----------|-------------|
| **Item Discount** | Single product | Different discount per item |
| **Bill Discount** | Entire invoice | Overall discount |
| **Scheme Discount** | Based on quantity | Buy 2 Get 1 Free type |
| **Customer Discount** | Customer-specific | Regular customer pricing |

---

## Item-Level Discount

Apply discount to individual products:

### Percentage Discount
1. Find the item in the invoice
2. Click the **Discount** column
3. Enter discount percentage (e.g., `10`)
4. Press Enter
5. Discount is calculated automatically

### Fixed Amount Discount
1. Click the **Discount** column
2. Enter amount with ₹ symbol (e.g., `₹50`)
3. Press Enter

---

## Bill-Level Discount

Apply discount to the total bill:

1. Scroll to the bottom of the invoice
2. Find **Additional Discount** field
3. Enter percentage or amount
4. Discount is applied to subtotal

> 💡 **Tip**: Bill discount is applied after item discounts.

---

## Discount Calculation Example

**Original Items**:
- Item A: ₹100 (10% item discount) = ₹90
- Item B: ₹200 (no discount) = ₹200
- Subtotal: ₹290

**Bill Discount**: 5%
- 5% of ₹290 = ₹14.50

**Final Amount**: ₹290 - ₹14.50 = ₹275.50 + GST

## Customer Default Discount

Set a default discount for regular customers:

1. Go to **Customers → Edit Customer**
2. Find **Default Discount** field
3. Enter the percentage
4. Save

Now this discount auto-applies when you select this customer!

---

## Scheme-Based Discounts

Set up promotional offers:

1. Go to **Settings → Schemes & Discounts**
2. Click **Add Scheme**
3. Choose type:
   - Buy X Get Y Free
   - Bundle discount
   - Quantity discount
4. Set products and conditions
5. Save scheme

Schemes apply automatically during billing when conditions are met.

---

## Discount Limits

Set maximum discount allowed:

1. Go to **Settings → Sales Settings**
2. Set **Maximum Discount %**
3. Users won't be able to exceed this

---

## Common Questions

### Can I give discount on MRP?
Discount is applied on selling price. The discounted price should not exceed MRP.

### Who can give discounts?
Based on user permissions. Managers typically have higher discount limits.

### How do I see all discounts given?
Check **Reports → Discount Report** for a summary.

### Can I block discount on certain products?
Yes, in product settings, you can set "Discount Allowed: No".

---

## Best Practices

✅ **Set discount limits** - Prevent excessive discounting  
✅ **Review discount reports** - Monitor discount patterns  
✅ **Use schemes** - For promotional discounts  
✅ **Document reasons** - Notes on why discount was given
