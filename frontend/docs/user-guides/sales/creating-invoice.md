# 🧾 Creating an Invoice

> Step-by-step guide to create a sales invoice.

---

## Overview

Creating an invoice is simple and takes just a few steps. Follow this guide to create your first invoice.

---

## Step 1: Open New Invoice

**Option A**: From Dashboard
- Click the **New Invoice** button on the dashboard

**Option B**: From Menu
- Go to **Sales → New Invoice**

---

## Step 2: Select Customer

1. In the **Customer** field, start typing:
   - Customer name, OR
   - Phone number, OR
   - Customer code

2. Select the customer from the dropdown

3. **New Customer?** Click **+ Add New** to create one quickly

> 💡 **Tip**: For walk-in customers, select "Walk-in Customer" or similar generic entry.

---

## Step 3: Add Products

### Search and Add
1. In the **Product Search** field:
   - Type product name, OR
   - Scan barcode using scanner, OR
   - Enter product code

2. Select the product from dropdown

3. The product is added to the invoice

### Edit Quantity
- Click on the **Qty** column
- Enter the quantity needed
- Press Enter or Tab

### Multiple Items
- Keep searching and adding more products
- Each product appears as a new line

---

## Step 4: Review Line Items

For each product, you'll see:

| Column | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Product Name** | Item description |
| **Batch** | Batch number & expiry |
| **Qty** | Quantity to sell |
| **Free** | Free items (if any) |
| **Rate** | Selling price per unit |
| **Discount** | Item discount (if any) |
| **GST** | Tax percentage |
| **Amount** | Line total |

### Editing
- Click any field to edit
- Delete items using the ✕ button

---

## Step 5: Apply Discounts (Optional)

### Item-Level Discount
- Click the **Discount** column for any item
- Enter discount % or amount

### Bill-Level Discount
- At the bottom, find **Additional Discount**
- Enter the overall discount

---

## Step 6: Select Payment Method

Choose how the customer is paying:

| Method | When to Use |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Customer pays in cash |
| **Card** | Debit/Credit card payment |
| **UPI** | Google Pay, PhonePe, etc. |
| **Credit** | Customer will pay later |
| **Split** | Part cash, part other |

### For Credit Sales
- The amount is added to customer's outstanding
- You can collect it later

### For Split Payments
- Enter cash amount
- Remaining goes to selected method

---

## Step 7: Add Notes (Optional)

- **Internal Notes** - For your reference only
- **Terms & Conditions** - Printed on invoice

---

## Step 8: Save Invoice

1. Click **Save Invoice** (or press Ctrl+S)
2. Invoice is created and saved
3. A success message appears with options

---

## Step 9: Print or Share

After saving, you can:

| Action | How |
|--------|-----|
| **Print** | Click Print or press Ctrl+P |
| **WhatsApp** | Click WhatsApp icon to share |
| **Download PDF** | Click Download |
| **Email** | Click Email to send |

---

## Common Questions

### Can I edit an invoice after saving?
Yes, if the invoice is in "Draft" status. Go to **Sales → Invoice History**, find the invoice, and click Edit.

### How do I apply GST?
GST is automatically calculated based on the product's tax rate. Ensure products have correct GST rates in their settings.

### What if I make a mistake?
- Draft invoices can be edited or deleted
- Confirmed invoices can be cancelled and re-created
- Use Sales Return for already-delivered goods

### How do I give discounts?
- **Item discount**: Click discount column on any item
- **Bill discount**: Use the additional discount field at bottom
