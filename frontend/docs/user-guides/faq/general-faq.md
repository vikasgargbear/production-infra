## Getting Started

### How do I log in for the first time?
1. You'll receive login credentials via email/SMS
2. Go to the application URL
3. Enter your username and password
4. You'll be prompted to change your password

### I forgot my password. What do I do?
1. Click **Forgot Password** on the login page
2. Enter your registered email or phone
3. You'll receive a reset link/OTP
4. Create a new password

### Can I use the app on mobile?
Yes! The application works on:
- Mobile browsers (Chrome, Safari)
- Tablet devices
- Desktop computers

## Sales & Invoicing

### How do I give a discount?
**Item Discount**: Click the discount column on any line item  
**Bill Discount**: Use the "Additional Discount" field at the bottom

### Can I edit an invoice after saving?
- **Draft invoices**: Yes, fully editable
- **Confirmed invoices**: Cannot edit, need to cancel and recreate
- **Paid invoices**: Use Sales Return for corrections

### How do I print in different sizes?
Go to **Settings → Print Templates** and select:
- Thermal (2" or 3")
- A4 size
- A5 size

### Customer not showing in search?
- Make sure you've added the customer
- Try searching by phone number
- Check if there are typos in the name

## Inventory

### How do I add stock?
Stock is added through:
1. **Purchase → Receive Goods (GRN)** - Normal way
2. **Inventory → Stock Adjustment** - For opening stock or corrections

### Why does my stock show negative?
This means a sale was made without sufficient stock. Check:
1. Was purchase entry missed?
2. Was GRN completed?
3. Create stock adjustment to correct

### How do I track expiry dates?
1. Enable batch tracking for the product
2. Enter expiry date when receiving goods
3. View expiring stock in **Inventory → Expiring Soon**

### How do I check low stock items?
Go to **Inventory → Current Stock** and click **Low Stock** filter. Or check the dashboard alerts.

## Payments

### How do I record a payment received?
1. Go to **Sales → Outstanding**
2. Find the customer
3. Click **Receive Payment**
4. Enter amount and method
5. Save

### What if customer pays more than due?
The excess becomes an advance/credit that's automatically applied to future invoices.

### Can I split payment between cash and UPI?
Yes! When creating invoice, select **Split Payment** and enter the cash portion. The rest goes to UPI/card.

## Reports

### Where can I see my daily sales?
Go to **Reports → Sales Report** and select today's date. Or check the Dashboard.

### How do I get my GST report?
Go to **Reports → GST Reports** and select the period. Download GSTR-1 format for filing.

### Can I export reports to Excel?
Yes! Every report has an **Export** button. Choose Excel or PDF format.

## Technical

### The app is running slow. What do I do?
1. Clear browser cache
2. Close other tabs
3. Check your internet connection
4. Try a different browser
5. Contact support if issue persists

### My data isn't syncing
1. Check internet connection
2. Click the Sync button manually
3. Log out and log back in
4. Contact support if issue continues

### Can multiple users work simultaneously?
Yes! Each user logs in with their own credentials. Data syncs in real-time.
