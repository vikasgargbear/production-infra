# 🚀 Quick Start Guide

> Get started with your business management software in just 5 minutes!

---

## Welcome! 

This guide will help you complete your first sale in under 5 minutes.

---

## Step 1: Log In

1. Open the application in your browser
2. Enter your **Username** (email or phone)
3. Enter your **Password**
4. Click **Log In**

> 💡 **Tip**: Check "Remember Me" to stay logged in on this device.

---

## Step 2: Explore the Dashboard

After logging in, you'll see the **Dashboard** with:

- **Today's Sales** - Your sales for the day
- **Pending Payments** - Outstanding dues from customers
- **Low Stock Alerts** - Items that need reordering
- **Quick Actions** - Buttons for common tasks

---

## Step 3: Create Your First Invoice

Let's create a sale:

### 3.1 Click "New Invoice"
- Find the **New Invoice** button on the dashboard
- Or go to **Sales → New Invoice** from the menu

### 3.2 Select Customer
- Type the customer's name or phone number
- Select from the dropdown
- Or click **+ New Customer** to add one

### 3.3 Add Products
- Search for products by name or scan barcode
- Enter the **quantity**
- The price and GST are calculated automatically

### 3.4 Choose Payment Method
- Select **Cash**, **Card**, **UPI**, or **Credit**
- For credit sales, the amount is added to customer's outstanding

### 3.5 Save & Print
- Click **Save Invoice**
- Choose to **Print** or share via **WhatsApp**

🎉 **Congratulations!** You've created your first invoice!

---

## Step 4: What's Next?

Now that you've made your first sale, explore more features:

| Want to... | Go to |
|------------|-------|
| Add more products | Inventory → Add Product |
| View sales history | Sales → Invoice History |
| Check stock levels | Inventory → Current Stock |
| Create purchase order | Purchase → New Order |
