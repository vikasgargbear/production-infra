# ⚙️ First-Time Setup

> Configure your account and business settings before you start.

---

## Before You Begin

Complete these one-time setup steps to get the most out of the application.

---

## Step 1: Update Company Profile

Set up your business information that appears on invoices:

1. Go to **Settings → Company Profile**
2. Fill in your details:
   - **Business Name** - Your company/shop name
   - **Address** - Complete business address
   - **Phone Number** - Customer contact number
   - **Email** - Business email
   - **GST Number** - Your GSTIN (if registered)
   - **Logo** - Upload your business logo

3. Click **Save Changes**

> 💡 **Tip**: Your GST number is required for generating GST-compliant invoices.

---

## Step 2: Configure Invoice Settings

Customize how your invoices look:

1. Go to **Settings → Invoice Settings**
2. Configure options:
   - **Invoice Prefix** - e.g., "INV-" or your shop initials
   - **Starting Number** - First invoice number
   - **Default Payment Terms** - Days until payment due
   - **Show Terms & Conditions** - Add default T&C

3. Click **Save**

---

## Step 3: Set Up Tax Rates (GST)

Configure your default tax rates:

1. Go to **Settings → Tax Configuration**
2. Review or add tax rates:
   - **GST 0%** - Exempt items
   - **GST 5%** - Essential items
   - **GST 12%** - Standard items
   - **GST 18%** - Most products
   - **GST 28%** - Luxury items

3. Set your **Default GST Rate** for new products

---

## Step 4: Add Your Products

Add products to your catalog:

1. Go to **Inventory → Add Product**
2. Enter product details:
   - **Product Name** - Clear, searchable name
   - **Product Code/SKU** - Unique identifier
   - **Category** - For organizing products
   - **MRP** - Maximum Retail Price
   - **Sale Price** - Your selling price
   - **GST Rate** - Applicable tax rate
   - **Reorder Level** - For low stock alerts

3. Click **Save Product**

> 💡 **Tip**: You can also bulk upload products from Excel.

---

## Step 5: Add Your Customers

Add your regular customers:

1. Go to **Customers → Add Customer**
2. Enter details:
   - **Name** - Customer/business name
   - **Phone** - Mobile number (used for search)
   - **Email** - Optional
   - **Address** - Delivery address
   - **GST Number** - For B2B customers

3. Click **Save Customer**

---

## Step 6: Add Team Members (Optional)

If you have staff who need access:

1. Go to **Settings → User Management**
2. Click **Add User**
3. Enter their details:
   - **Name**
   - **Email/Phone** - For login
   - **Role** - Admin, Manager, Salesperson, etc.
   - **Permissions** - What they can access

4. They'll receive login credentials
