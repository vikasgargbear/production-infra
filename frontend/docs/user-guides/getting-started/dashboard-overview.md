# 📊 Dashboard Overview

> Learn how to navigate and use your dashboard effectively.

---

## What is the Dashboard?

The Dashboard is your home screen - it gives you a quick overview of your business at a glance. Every time you log in, you'll see the most important information about your business.

---

## Dashboard Sections

### 📈 Key Metrics (Top Row)

At the top, you'll see your key business numbers:

| Metric | What it Shows |
|--------|---------------|
| **Today's Sales** | Total sales amount for today |
| **This Month's Sales** | Sales for the current month |
| **Pending Payments** | Outstanding dues from customers |
| **Low Stock Items** | Products below reorder level |

> 💡 **Tip**: Click on any metric to see more details.

---

### ⚡ Quick Actions

Large buttons for the tasks you do most often:

- **New Invoice** - Create a new sale
- **New Challan** - Delivery without invoice
- **New Purchase** - Order from supplier
- **Record Payment** - Collect customer payment

---

### 🔔 Alerts & Notifications

Important things that need your attention:

- **Low Stock Alert** - Products running low
- **Expiry Alert** - Products expiring soon
- **Payment Due** - Customer payments overdue
- **Order Updates** - Purchase order status changes

> 💡 **Tip**: Click on any alert to take action immediately.

---

### 📋 Recent Invoices

Your last few sales:

- See invoice number, customer name, and amount
- Click any invoice to view details
- Quickly print or share recent invoices

---

### 📊 Sales Chart

A visual graph showing:

- Daily sales trend
- Compare with previous periods
- Identify peak sales days

You can switch between:
- **Daily** view
- **Weekly** view
- **Monthly** view

---

## Navigation Menu

On the left side (or top on mobile), you'll find the main menu:

| Menu Item | What it Does |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Dashboard** | Return to this home screen |
| **Sales** | Invoices, orders, returns |
| **Purchase** | Purchase orders, receive goods |
| **Inventory** | Products, stock, categories |
| **Customers** | Customer list and details |
| **Suppliers** | Supplier list and details |
| **Reports** | Sales, stock, GST reports |
| **Settings** | Configure your account |

---

## Using the Search Bar

The search bar at the top helps you find anything quickly:

- **Search customers** - Type a name or phone number
- **Search products** - Type product name or code
- **Search invoices** - Type invoice number

Just start typing and results appear instantly!

---

## Keyboard Shortcuts

For faster navigation:

| Shortcut | Action |
|----------|--------|
| `Ctrl + /` | Show all shortcuts |
| `Ctrl + I` | New Invoice |
| `Ctrl + P` | Print current page |
| `Ctrl + S` | Save current form |
| `/` | Focus search bar |

---

## Customizing Your Dashboard

You can personalize what you see:

1. Click the **⚙️ Settings** icon on the dashboard
2. Choose which widgets to show/hide
3. Rearrange sections by dragging
4. Set your preferred date range
