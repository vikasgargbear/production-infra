# 📊 Sales Reports

> Analyze your sales performance with detailed reports.

---

## Available Sales Reports

| Report | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Sales Summary** | Total sales by period |
| **Sales by Customer** | Customer-wise breakdown |
| **Sales by Product** | Product-wise breakdown |
| **Sales by Category** | Category performance |
| **Daily Sales** | Day-by-day details |
| **Salesperson Report** | Employee performance |

---

## Accessing Reports

1. Go to **Reports → Sales**
2. Select the report type
3. Choose date range
4. Click **Generate**

---

## Sales Summary Report

Overview of your sales:

### What it Shows
- Total sales value
- Number of invoices
- Average invoice value
- GST collected
- Discounts given

### How to Use
1. Select date range
2. View summary totals
3. Click any metric to drill down

---

## Sales by Customer

Identify your best customers:

### What it Shows
- Customer name
- Number of purchases
- Total sales value
- Average order value
- Outstanding balance

### How to Use
- Sort by sales value to find top customers
- Identify declining customers
- Plan customer outreach

---

## Sales by Product

See which products sell best:

### What it Shows
- Product name
- Quantity sold
- Total sales value
- Profit margin
- Stock remaining

### How to Use
- Identify best sellers
- Find slow-moving items
- Plan purchasing decisions

---

## Filtering Reports

Narrow down your data:

| Filter | Options |
|--------|---------|
| **Date Range** | Today, This Week, Month, Custom |
| **Customer** | Specific customer |
| **Product** | Specific product |
| **Category** | Product category |
| **Salesperson** | Staff member |
| **Payment Status** | Paid, Pending, All |

---

## Exporting Reports

Save or share your reports:

1. Generate the report
2. Click **Export**
3. Choose format:
   - **Excel** - For further analysis
   - **PDF** - For sharing/printing
4. File downloads to your device

---

## Printing Reports

1. Generate the report
2. Click **Print**
3. Select printer
4. Print

---

## Common Questions

### Why are my totals different from invoices?
Check the date range and filters. Also ensure all invoices are confirmed (drafts may not be included).

### Can I compare periods?
Generate reports for each period and compare, or use the comparison feature if available.

### How do I see returns?
Returns are typically shown separately or as negative values. Check Sales Returns report.
