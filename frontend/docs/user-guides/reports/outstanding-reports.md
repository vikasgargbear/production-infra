# 💰 Outstanding Reports

> Track receivables and payables with outstanding reports.

---

## Types of Outstanding

| Type | Shows |
|------|-------|
| **Receivables** | Money customers owe you |
| **Payables** | Money you owe suppliers |

---

## Receivables Report

### What it Shows
- Customer name
- Total outstanding
- Overdue amount
- Number of invoices
- Days since oldest due

### Aging Analysis
Break down by how overdue:
- Current (not yet due)
- 1-30 days overdue
- 31-60 days overdue
- 61-90 days overdue
- Over 90 days overdue

### How to Access
1. Go to **Reports → Outstanding**
2. Select **Receivables**
3. Choose date range
4. Generate report

---

## Payables Report

### What it Shows
- Supplier name
- Total outstanding
- Overdue amount
- Payment due dates
- Aging breakdown

### How to Access
1. Go to **Reports → Outstanding**
2. Select **Payables**
3. Choose date range
4. Generate report

---

## Aging Report Details

### What it Shows

| Aging Bucket | Urgency |
|-------------|---------|
| **Current** | Not urgent, not due yet |
| **1-30 Days** | Follow up soon |
| **31-60 Days** | Need attention |
| **61-90 Days** | High priority |
| **Over 90 Days** | Critical - may become bad debt |

### Using Aging Data
- Focus collection on older dues first
- Identify customers with payment issues
- Plan cash flow based on expected collections

---

## Customer-Wise Outstanding

Detailed view per customer:

1. Click on customer in report
2. See all unpaid invoices
3. Invoice dates and amounts
4. Days overdue for each

---

## Exporting Reports

1. Generate the report
2. Click **Export**
3. Choose format:
   - **Excel** - For analysis or sharing with collection team
   - **PDF** - For management review

---

## Sending Collection Reminders

From the report:
1. Select customer(s)
2. Click **Send Reminder**
3. Choose WhatsApp, SMS, or Email
4. Message is sent automatically

---

## Common Questions

### Why does outstanding not match ledger?
Check for unapplied payments or missing invoices. Reconcile both.

### Can I see payment history?
Click on customer to see all payments received.

### How do I write off bad debt?
Contact admin to create write-off entry. Records are maintained.
