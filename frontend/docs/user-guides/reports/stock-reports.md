# 📦 Stock Reports

> Understand your inventory with stock reports.

---

## Available Stock Reports

| Report | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **Current Stock** | Real-time stock levels |
| **Stock Valuation** | Value of inventory |
| **Stock Movement** | In/Out transactions |
| **Low Stock** | Items below reorder |
| **Expiring Stock** | Items near expiry |
| **Dead Stock** | No movement items |

---

## Current Stock Report

See all stock levels:

### What it Shows
- Product name
- Category
- Available quantity
- Reserved quantity
- Reorder level
- Stock status

### Filters
- **Category** - Filter by product category
- **Location** - Specific location
- **Status** - Low/Normal/Overstocked

---

## Stock Valuation Report

Know the value of your inventory:

### What it Shows
- Product name
- Quantity in stock
- Unit cost
- Total value
- Percentage of inventory

### Valuation Methods
Stock is valued at purchase cost (typically weighted average).

---

## Stock Movement Report

Track where stock goes:

### What it Shows
- Date and time
- Transaction type (Sale, Purchase, Adjustment, etc.)
- Quantity in/out
- Running balance
- Reference document

### How to Use
1. Select date range
2. Filter by product or category
3. See all movements

---

## Expiring Stock Report

Manage expiry dates:

### What it Shows
- Product and batch
- Expiry date
- Days until expiry
- Quantity remaining
- Value at risk

### How to Use
- Set expiry window (e.g., 30, 60, 90 days)
- Review items regularly
- Plan clearance sales

---

## Low Stock Report

Items needing reorder:

### What it Shows
- Product name
- Current stock
- Reorder level
- Shortage quantity
- Last purchase date

### How to Use
- Review daily
- Create purchase orders
- Adjust reorder levels

---

## Exporting Stock Reports

1. Generate the report
2. Click **Export**
3. Choose **Excel** or **PDF**
4. File downloads

---

## Common Questions

### Stock doesn't match physical count?
Check for unrecorded sales, pending GRNs, or need stock adjustment.

### How is valuation calculated?
Typically at average purchase cost. Check Settings for your method.

### Can I see historical stock?
Stock movement report shows history. Point-in-time stock requires date filter.
