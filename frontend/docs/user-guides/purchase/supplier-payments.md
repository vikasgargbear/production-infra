# 💸 Supplier Payments

> Learn how to record payments to your suppliers.

---

## Overview

Track what you owe suppliers and record payments to keep accounts accurate.

---

## View Supplier Outstanding

See what you owe:

1. Go to **Purchase → Supplier Outstanding**
2. View list of suppliers with pending payments
3. Click a supplier to see invoice breakdown

---

## Recording a Payment

### Method 1: From Outstanding

1. Go to **Purchase → Supplier Outstanding**
2. Find the supplier
3. Click **Make Payment**
4. Enter payment details:
   - **Amount** - How much paying
   - **Date** - Payment date
   - **Method** - Cash/Bank Transfer/Cheque
   - **Reference** - Transaction ref if applicable
5. Click **Save Payment**

### Method 2: From Purchase Invoice

1. Go to **Purchase → Purchase History**
2. Find the specific invoice
3. Click **Record Payment**
4. Enter amount and details
5. Save

---

## Payment Methods

| Method | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Cash** | Physical cash payment |
| **Bank Transfer** | NEFT/RTGS/IMPS |
| **Cheque** | Bank cheque |
| **UPI** | Google Pay, PhonePe, etc. |

---

## Partial Payments

You can pay less than full amount:

1. Enter the partial amount
2. Save payment
3. Remaining stays as outstanding
4. Pay more anytime

---

## Advance Payments

Paying before receiving goods:

1. Go to **Finance → Payments → New Payment**
2. Select **Payment Out**
3. Choose supplier
4. Enter amount as **Advance**
5. Save

When goods are received, advance is adjusted.

---

## Viewing Payment History

See all payments made:

1. Go to **Finance → Payments**
2. Filter by **Payment Out**
3. Search by supplier name
4. See payment details and dates

---

## Common Questions

### How do I know what I owe?
Check **Purchase → Supplier Outstanding** for summary by supplier.

### Can I pay multiple invoices at once?
Yes, enter total amount. It's allocated to oldest invoices first.

### What if I overpaid?
Creates an advance/credit with the supplier. Applied to next purchase.

### How do I track cheques?
When entering payment:
- Select Cheque as method
- Enter cheque number
- Enter cheque date
- Mark as cleared when it's encashed

---

## Best Practices

✅ **Record promptly** - Enter payments when made  
✅ **Keep references** - Note transaction IDs  
✅ **Review outstanding** - Check weekly  
✅ **Match with bank** - Reconcile regularly
