# 🛒 Creating a Purchase Order

> Learn how to create purchase orders for your suppliers.

---

## What is a Purchase Order?

A **Purchase Order (PO)** is a formal request to your supplier for products. It helps:
- Track what you ordered
- Ensure correct delivery
- Maintain supplier records

---

## Step 1: Start New Purchase

1. Go to **Purchase → New Purchase Order**
2. Or from Dashboard → **Add Purchase** quick action

---

## Step 2: Select Supplier

1. In the **Supplier** field, search by:
   - Supplier name
   - Phone number
   - Code

2. Select from dropdown

3. **New Supplier?** Click **+ Add New**

---

## Step 3: Add Products

### Search and Add
1. Type product name or code
2. Select from dropdown
3. Product is added to order

### Enter Details
For each product:
- **Quantity** - How many to order
- **Rate** - Purchase price per unit
- **Discount** - If applicable
- **Free Qty** - If supplier offers free goods

---

## Step 4: Additional Details

Fill optional information:

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **PO Date** | Order creation date |
| **Expected Date** | When you expect delivery |
| **Reference** | Supplier quote number |
| **Notes** | Special instructions |

---

## Step 5: Review & Save

1. Check all items and quantities
2. Verify totals
3. Click **Save Purchase Order**

---

## What Happens Next?

### Draft PO
- Not yet sent to supplier
- Can be edited freely

### Confirmed PO
- Share with supplier
- Print or email

### When Goods Arrive
- Create GRN (Goods Receipt Note)
- Stock is added to inventory

---

## Printing / Sharing PO

After saving:
1. Click **Print** for physical copy
2. Click **Email** to send to supplier
3. Click **WhatsApp** to share instantly

---

## PO Status Tracking

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Draft** | Not confirmed |
| **Confirmed** | Sent to supplier |
| **Partially Received** | Some goods arrived |
| **Fully Received** | All goods received |
| **Cancelled** | Order cancelled |

---

## Common Questions

### Does PO affect my stock?
No, stock only increases when you do GRN (receive goods).

### Can I edit a confirmed PO?
You can add notes, but quantities shouldn't change after sending to supplier.

### What if prices change?
Update during GRN - actual receipt can differ from PO.
