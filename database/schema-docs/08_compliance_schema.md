# Compliance Schema Documentation

**Schema:** `compliance`
**Purpose:** Pharmaceutical regulatory compliance management
**Last Updated:** 2025-10-16
**Tables:** 28 (largest schema)

---

## Overview

The `compliance` schema manages pharmaceutical regulatory compliance including drug licenses, narcotic/psychotropic substance tracking, inspections, regulatory audits, environmental compliance, quality certifications, and legal documentation. Critical for pharma industry operations.

---

## Table Categories

### License Management (6 tables)
Drug licenses, renewals, types, authority management, compliance tracking, documentation

### Narcotic/Psychotropic Tracking (5 tables)
Schedule H/H1/X substance tracking, register maintenance, disposal records, audit trail

### Inspections & Audits (4 tables)
Regulatory inspections, findings, corrective actions, follow-ups

### Quality Certifications (3 tables)
ISO/WHO-GMP/GLP certifications, renewal tracking, audit schedules

### Product Compliance (4 tables)
Product registrations, label approvals, formulation compliance, contraindications

### Environmental Compliance (3 tables)
Waste management, effluent treatment, pollution control board compliance

### Legal & Documentation (3 tables)
Legal cases, regulatory correspondence, compliance calendar

---

## Key Features

- **Drug License Management**: Multi-state license tracking with auto-renewal alerts
- **Narcotic Register**: Daily register maintenance per legal requirements
- **Inspection Tracking**: Complete inspection lifecycle from scheduling to closure
- **Certification Management**: ISO/GMP certificate validity tracking
- **Product Registration**: State-wise product registration and renewal
- **Waste Management**: Biomedical/pharma waste disposal compliance
- **Audit Trail**: Complete compliance activity logging
